To simplify life, I decided to standardize the IT solutions I have across fewer platforms. I transitioned the WordPress sites and am now consolidating the AI platforms I learned last month.  I will standardize on Anthropic Claude, GitHub, Cloudflare, Supabase, Stripe, and Svetlia CMS.

In my most recent project, I converted my first ChatGPT Pages attempt, the [**IT4IT Altas**](https://javc.nl/it4it-atlas/), to Anthropic Claude and integrated it into my website, JAvC.nl. 

The solution:

- Surveys the websites of relevant software vendors and gathers (and updates) their Software solutions.
- Automatically maps the discovered solutions to IT4IT value chains, CSDM 5 Domains, and TBM 5 Solution types.
- Automatically categorizes the solutions in a mix of the practices above.
- Automatically updates the 1.6000 tools that it holds (ZERO TOUCH).
- Provides visitors to JAvC.nl with a user experience that lets them query, filter, and export a filtered subset of the tool list.

To keep the scope reasonable and relevant for large enterprise use, Atlas does not include personal software packages designed for PCs or Phones. 

The site automatically seeks and processes updates using API's and AI. To keep AI token costs reasonable, the site updates once per week. In the Changelog, you can find all the changes made last week. 

If you find IT4IT Atlas useful, and/or if it has saved you days of survey work, you can contribute and sponsor the AI tokens that IT4IT Atlas consumes by clicking the Tikkie QR code. Sponsors can opt in to the weekly updates via email.
