Since the beginning of this decade, I have promoted using the TBM Council's definitions and content as the basis for IT Service/Operations Management. I believe commodity services in the digital portfolio should be structured using commodity taxonomy and should be linked to discovered/federated Configuration items. Between these two layers, there should be an offerings layer specific to groups of subscribers, with DevOps teams innovating and maintaining these offerings.  For non-commodity services (i.e., the company's core business), enterprises should apply a similar data model, but with differentiated services and offerings.

When integrating CSDM5 and TBM5, you have both the data model and the commodity taxonomy.  By relating and mapping the discovered/federated CIs to the defined offerings, you can automate risk and impact analysis, and ratings, ownership, and management of the CIs may be inherited from the Digital (Application & Service) Portfolio.

This idea formed the basis of the [**White Paper**](https://www.servicenow.com/community/s/cgfwn76974/attachments/cgfwn76974/common-service-data-model-forum/7406/1/TBM%20Integration%20with%20ServiceNow%20CSDM%20\(whitepaper\).pdf) I drafted in 2023 with a group of like-minded folks from the TBM council and the ServiceNow Community.  The announcement strengthened ServiceNow and the TBM Council's collaboration.
