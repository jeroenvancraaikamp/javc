The IT4IT Atlas is a directory of IT management tools, business applications, workplace platforms and technology platforms. The IT4IT tools are grouped by [business capabilities](/it4it-atlas/categories/) and mapped to the value streams of the [IT4IT 3 standard](https://www.opengroup.org/it4it) and the [CSDM 5 standard](https://www.servicenow.com/docs/r/servicenow-platform/common-service-data-model-csdm/csdm-landing-page.html); business applications follow the [TBM 5 categories](https://www.tbmcouncil.org/taxonomy/). Search, filter and export the list to Excel.
